Turnip Quest (Mesa Turnip, the open-source Vulkan driver for Adreno GPUs)

Built for Meta Quest 3 by quest/turnip/build-turnip-quest.sh from Mesa
26.3.0-devel (git a995eaeda4, the base of Turnip Gen8 V37) with
quest/turnip/tu-kgsl-gpuobj-quest.patch (buffers allocated through the
KGSL GPUOBJ ioctls: Horizon OS refuses GPUMEM_ALLOC_ID/FREE_ID to apps, so
stock Turnip fails at vkCreateDevice there) and the Android gralloc fixes of
StevenMXZ's Turnip Gen8 V37 recipe. Pinyon Shift bundles it as the
recommended driver on VR headsets; android_gpu_driver chooses another
driver or the system's.

Mesa source: https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa
